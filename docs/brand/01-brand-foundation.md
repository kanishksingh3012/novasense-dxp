# NovaSense — Brand foundation

Status: direction v1. Primary audience confirmed by the user on 2026-10-21; remaining brand and product choices are proposals for discussion. No customer journeys, adaptation rules, or application implementation are approved by this document.

## The central idea

NovaSense helps people make sense of their cycle through a discreet wearable and clear, considerate guidance.

The fictional product combines a patch, an app subscription, and optional coaching. The prototype demonstrates an imagined product experience using simulated readings. The brand must feel authentic without inventing clinical evidence, regulatory approvals, partnerships, or customer testimonials.

## Website and app scope

Confirmed by the user on 2026-10-21: improving the website UX is a core focus. The experience includes visitors browsing the brand, learning about the product, and deciding whether it fits their needs. The mobile app is part of the wider offering.

The public website must make the brand understandable before asking visitors to buy: what NovaSense is, who it is for, how the proposed product works, what is included, and what ongoing use involves. Brand imagery, product explanations, navigation, and purchase consideration are first-class design work. The customer app carries the ongoing cycle-awareness experience. Detailed journeys and website adaptation rules remain for the next phase.

## Confirmed primary audience

Primary: adults who track their menstrual cycle and want a clearer understanding of their personal patterns without constantly interpreting disconnected numbers.

User-selected focus: “Cycle awareness and understanding personal patterns.” Lead the brand story with learning about one's own cycle, noticing patterns over time, and connecting observations with personal check-ins. Evaluate copy and imagery against this focus.

Secondary: people preparing for conception who want to organize observations and questions for professional conversations. This is a proposed later use case, not a fertility prediction promise.

PCOS management and performance optimization remain outside the initial brand story. Discuss their inclusion later rather than making one homepage promise everything to everyone.

## Positioning

For people looking for clarity in their cycle, NovaSense is a wearable companion that turns a stream of observations into an understandable daily picture, with guidance that leaves room for individual experience.

Brand promise: clarity with care.

Working headline: **A clearer sense of your cycle.**

Supporting copy: **A discreet patch. Your patterns, brought together. Guidance for the day ahead.**

## Personality

- Considerate: acknowledge how someone feels before presenting an action.
- Precise: distinguish observations, estimates, and unknowns.
- Composed: use restrained visual emphasis and short, useful explanations.
- Human: write like a thoughtful guide, without synthetic cheerfulness.

## Voice in practice

| Situation | Example copy |
| --- | --- |
| Product introduction | A clearer sense of your cycle. |
| Learning period | Your baseline is taking shape. |
| Missing readings | Your patch hasn’t synced yet. Let’s check the connection. |
| Incomplete information | There isn’t enough information to show a pattern yet. |
| Check-in | How are you feeling today? |
| Support | Talk it through with a coach. |

Avoid: “perfect hormones,” “fix your cycle,” guaranteed outcomes, pressure to conceive, unsupported precision, and messaging that blames a customer for disengaging.

## Visual identity: Clinical Earth

Preserve the original brief’s palette. These are brand roles, not a completed accessible UI token system.

| Color | Hex | Intended role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plaster | #F9F8F6 | Main surfaces, editorial backgrounds |
| Sage | #A3B19B | Brand accent, soft panels, product packaging |
| Sand | #D3BBA5 | Warm supporting surfaces, material details |
| Slate | #697A8D | Supporting graphics, clinical accents |
| Charcoal | #333333 | Primary text, wordmark |

Use charcoal for body text on light surfaces. Do not assume sage, sand, or slate are suitable for small text or white button labels; check contrast during design-system work. Do not encode biological or emotional states through color alone.

Typography direction: Newsreader for short editorial headings; Geist Sans for interface text. Evaluate font delivery and licensing during implementation. Keep chart values and controls in the sans-serif family.

Logo direction: a restrained typographic “NovaSense” wordmark. No medical cross, molecular cliché, or decorative fertility symbol. A secondary symbol can follow after the name treatment is accepted; generated packaging lettering is a mockup, not master logo artwork.

Photography: tactile product materials, soft daylight, realistic scale, spacious compositions, natural skin texture, ordinary domestic settings. Avoid futuristic glowing sensors, floating holograms, and staged laboratory authority.

## Authenticity through detail

Build credibility through consistent packaging, onboarding language, product dimensions, support information, transparent explanations, and sensible empty states. Do not manufacture reviews, certifications, research statistics, clinician endorsements, or a company history.
